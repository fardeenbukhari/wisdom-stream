WISDOM STREAM - Watch together, in sync
=======================================

START
1. Open "Wisdom Stream" from your Desktop or Start menu.
2. A black window opens and your browser opens Wisdom Stream automatically.
   Keep the black window open. Closing it stops the app.
3. If Windows Firewall asks: tick "Private networks" and click "Allow".
   (This lets friends on the same Wi-Fi join.)

WATCH PARTY
1. Open a movie and click "Start watch party".
2. Copy the link and open it on a friend's phone/laptop (same Wi-Fi or hotspot).
3. Friend types a name and clicks "Join party".
4. You play / pause / skip and everyone follows. Use the chat on the right.

OWN VIDEOS (works without internet)
Start menu > Wisdom Stream > "My Videos Folder". Put .mp4 files there,
then refresh the home page.

PROBLEMS
- "Port 9090 is already in use": the app is already running. Open http://localhost:9090
- Friends can't open the link: put everyone on the same phone hotspot.

UNINSTALL
Settings > Apps > Wisdom Stream > Uninstall
